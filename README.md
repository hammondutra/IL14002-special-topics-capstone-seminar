# IL14002 — Special Topics Capstone Seminar

**Minerva University · Fall 2026**

This repository is a public-facing archive of writing produced for **IL14002: Special Topics Capstone Seminar**.

The course brings together philosophical questions about AI — including authorship, agency, consciousness, authenticity, honesty, identity, cognition, and creativity — with practical work in public writing, editing, revision, and peer workshop. A recurring focus of the seminar is not only what AI can do for writers, but what its use changes about voice, responsibility, interpretation, and the writing process itself.

## Published work

### Assignment 2 — Film Review
**“Vulnerability as a Transaction”**  
A philosophy- and psychology-oriented review of *Black Mirror: White Christmas*, focused on vulnerability, connection, isolation, and the way technology turns existing human needs into systems.

The current website opens directly to this review.

## Course themes

Among the themes developed throughout the seminar are:

- AI, authorship, and agency
- AI and learning
- authenticity and disclosure
- AI and identity
- emotions and emotional outsourcing
- writing and cognition
- justice and power
- creativity and novelty
- public audience, evidence, sourcing, and writing ethics
- editing partnerships and iterative revision
